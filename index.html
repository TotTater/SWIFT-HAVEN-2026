How to prepare your character

Put your character's images into one folder. The two required images must have these exact names:

neutral.png
talking.png

Optional expressions

pleased.png
sad.png
angry.png
shocked.png
disappointed.png

PNG, JPG, JPEG and WEBP images are supported. Keep the filenames exactly as shown.

Missing optional expressions will automatically use your neutral image.
